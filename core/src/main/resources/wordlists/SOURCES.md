# Word list: where it comes from, its licence and the change made to it

`eff_short_wordlist_1.txt` holds the words of the suggested master password (ux.md §5.3) and of the generator's "Words" mode (ux.md §5.15): the Electronic Frontier Foundation's short word list 1, with one line removed (architecture.md D-6a). It is read by `core.generator.Wordlist`. Brought into the repository by build-plan package 2.6 on 2026-10-07.

- **Origin:** EFF's "general short word list (for use with four dice)", `https://www.eff.org/files/2016/09/08/eff_short_wordlist_1.txt`, published with the article "Deep Dive: EFF's New Wordlists for Random Passphrases" by Joseph Bonneau, 19 July 2016, `https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2016/07/new-wordlists-random-passphrases`. Downloaded on 2026-10-07: 1,296 lines `<four dice digits><TAB><word>` with LF line ends, 13,660 bytes, SHA-256 `8f5ca830b8bffb6fe39c9736c024a00a6a6411adb3f83a9be8bfeeb6e067ae69`.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), `https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`. EFF's copyright policy (`https://www.eff.org/copyright`): "Any and all original material on the EFF website may be freely distributed at will under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC-BY), unless otherwise noted." Copyright Electronic Frontier Foundation. The list is provided as it is, without warranties (CC BY 4.0, section 5).
- **Change:** the line `6652<TAB>yo-yo` was removed. "yo-yo" is the list's only word with a hyphen, and the hyphen is the separator between the words of a passphrase, so it would read as two words. Nothing else was changed: the other 1,295 lines, their dice numbers, their order and their line ends are EFF's. Because the dice number 6652 is now missing, the file no longer serves a roll of four dice; the app draws every word with `SecureRandom` instead.
- **Result:** 1,295 distinct words of 3 to 5 letters a–z (4.5 on average), in ascending order; 13,649 bytes, SHA-256 `992a27c7ed099b3c7cc444b5137a613ea80665f0ab6dfd78a3c445f33dc3fa79`. Six words give 6 × log2(1,295) = 62.0 bits.
- **Credit in the app:** the licences screen (build-plan package 7.3) names the list, EFF, the licence with its link, and this change (architecture.md §7.1, VERIFICATION.md V-C13).

`core/src/test/java/…/core/generator/WordlistTest.kt` checks both SHA-256 sums above: the file's own, and EFF's after the removed line is put back in its place.
